# UDYOGA — Official 2-Minute Jury Pitch Script
**Role:** Technical Lead & Agentic AI Architect  
**Target Duration:** Exactly 120 seconds (2 minutes)  
**Spoken Word Count:** ~245 words (~125–130 words per minute cadence)  
**Tone:** Confident, precise, technically authoritative founder

---

## The 2-Minute Spoken Script

> *"Judges, in India today, over 63 million MSMEs form the backbone of our economy—yet 85% of them are rejected or under-funded by traditional banks. Why? Because conventional credit appraisal demands three years of audited balance sheets, physical collateral, and a mature bureau score that small businesses simply don’t have.*
>
> *We built **UDYOGA: the Autonomous, Resilience-First Underwriting Protocol for Indian MSMEs**.*
>
> *Instead of relying on a single generic AI chatbot or a black-box bureau score, UDYOGA deploys **seven coordinated, specialized intelligence agents** operating on a deterministic state machine.*
>
> *First, our **Financial Intelligence** and **Business Vitality** agents extract an enterprise’s **Financial DNA** directly from high-frequency UPI and banking telemetry—measuring cash velocity, revenue volatility, and counterparty concentration.*
>
> *Next, our **Transaction Forensics** agent runs NetworkX topological graph analysis to detect circular fund round-tripping, while our **Evidence Verification** agent audits data provenance under DPDP Act principles.*
>
> *Here is our breakthrough: **Resilience-first underwriting**. Our **Resilience Simulation** agent stresses the business’s cash flow against realistic economic shocks—a 30% demand contraction, raw material inflation, or 30-day payment delays. We don't ask how much they made yesterday; we mathematically prove how much debt they can service under distress.*
>
> *Finally, our **Underwriting** and **Explanation** agents synthesize this into a bank-ready, 5-section **Credit Appraisal Memo (CAM)**—complete with safe loan principal caps, daily UPI sweeps, and a SHA-256 audit seal.*
>
> *With UDYOGA, lending moves from backward-looking collateral to forward-looking cash-flow resilience. Thank you."*

## Delivery Tips for Hackathon Stage

1. **Pacing:** Speak at a steady conversational pace. Do not rush. Let the terms *"Financial DNA"* and *"Resilience-First"* land with deliberate pauses.
2. **Body Language:** When you say *"seven coordinated agents"*, gesture toward the screen where the 7-agent execution DAG is visible.
3. **Emphasis:** Emphasize the word **"Deterministic"**—hackathon juries often worry about LLM hallucinations in financial applications. Pointing out that math is 100% deterministic gives instant technical credibility.
4. **Closing Eye Contact:** Look directly at the lead judge for the final punchline: *"from backward-looking collateral to forward-looking cash-flow resilience."*
