## 2. Critical ACIE Architectural Boundary Adherence

As stipulated in the project charter:
- **Zero Frontend Scoring Duplication:** The frontend does not calculate credit scores or execute machine learning algorithms independently.
- **Structured Assessment Display:** The workbench consumes structured outputs (credit vitality score 0–900, confidence score 94.2%, SHAP feature weights, and anomaly flags) generated by the teammate-owned Alternative Credit Intelligence Engine (ACIE).
- **Clear Simulated Labeling:** All mock inputs and synthetic transaction logs are explicitly annotated (`Simulated Sandbox Telemetry`, `ACIE Inferred Output`).
- **Human Decision Primacy:** The automated engine only provides an *indicative recommendation*; the human credit officer retains final discretionary authority to approve, adjust haircuts, mandate covenants, request clarifications, or decline.

## 3. Detailed Component Architecture

### 3.1 Multi-Stream Triangulation Radar
- **3-Way Congruence:** Automatically reconciles:
  - Bank Inflows (Account Aggregator): ₹5,84,000/mo.
  - GST Returns (GSTR-1 & 3B): ₹6,10,000/mo.
  - UPI Merchant QR Velocity: ₹5,12,000/mo (142 daily settlements).
- **Congruence Rating:** 95.8% Match (within tolerable ±10% retail band).

### 3.2 Cash-Flow & Expense Visualisations
- **30-Day Inflow vs Outflow Waveform:** Responsive Recharts `AreaChart` with dual gradient layers.
- **Expense Breakdown:** Recharts `PieChart` categorizing supplier settlements (68%), commercial rent (12%), utility debits (8%), and net margin (12%).
- **Financial Activity Timeline:** Chronological log of recurring supplier settlements, rent debits, and weekend revenue spikes.

### 3.3 Anomaly & Risk Detection Matrix
- **Circular Loop Detector:** Inspects transaction graphs to verify 0 artificial round-tripping.
- **Customer Concentration Gauge:** Confirms top customer constitutes only 18% of revenue.
- **Velocity Spike Anomaly:** Validates zero synthetic pre-application inflation.

### 3.4 Query & Document Request Workflow
- In-app query composer allowing credit officers to dispatch formal clarification requests directly to the borrower via SMS notification.

### 3.5 Underwriting Worksheet & Human Decision Interface
- **Risk Haircut Slider:** Dynamic calibration of the sanction limit (0% to 30%).
- **Mandatory Covenants:** Automatic inclusion of UPI AutoPay daily sweep covenants and quarterly stock audits.
- **Decision Engine:** One-click generation of immutable digital Sanction Letters or regulatory decline notices.
